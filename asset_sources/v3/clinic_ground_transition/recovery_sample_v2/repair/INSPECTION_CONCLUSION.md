## What the saved data proves

1. **The final normal texture uses the intended UV and color space.** All three Image Texture vector inputs come from the explicit `BakeUV` UV Map. The Tangent Normal Map also names `BakeUV`; the normal image is Non-Color. Both LowGround and LowFull have BakeUV active and active-render. This rules out the proposed wrong-UV/final-node wiring explanation for this saved preview.
2. **Wrong normals are already inside the native baked texture, not only in empty padding.** Rasterizing all 9,578 low triangles at the actual 1,024² pixel centers found 543,605 strictly interior occupied pixels across 57 islands and no strictly interior pixel-center overlap. There are 1,550 top, 396 side and 19 solid pixels with tangent Z below zero. This sign is a diagnostic for this closely matched surface pair, not a universal validity rule for arbitrary normal maps.
3. **Adjacent-island gutter pollution cannot explain the main top failure.** All 1,550 bad top pixels are on pure-top island 3. Only 40 (2.58%) have another occupied island within the original 8 px neighborhood. A further 596 belong to triangles whose geometric neighbors are only top and have no other island nearby. At pixel **(37,139), bottom-origin**, UV **(0.03662109375,0.13623046875)**, triangle **7521**, the stored normal is **(0.611764729,0.509803951,0.011764707)**: approximately **167.06°** from tangent +Z, with **AO=1**. This is not an AO-darkened texel or a neighboring side island blended by the final linear filter.
4. **The retained earlier join check does not support a final-mesh tangent flip.** All 8,960 ground triangles match LowFull positions and BakeUV; all 26,880 corners match sign, with normal/tangent numerical angle differences below 0.04°. High and low deliberately have different tessellation because of real high subdivision; their triangle counts are not claimed identical. The earlier independent cage rays hit the expected face class, but those rays were reconstructed externally, not captured from Cycles. They therefore do not prove what the original bake actually hit.
5. **Side source-UV failure is independently established.** All 512 vertical ground-closure triangles have zero-area RockScanUV and SoilScanUV because the source used horizontal XZ mapping on these vertical faces. This explains the visible streaking irrespective of the normal switch. Their BakeUV is nonzero.

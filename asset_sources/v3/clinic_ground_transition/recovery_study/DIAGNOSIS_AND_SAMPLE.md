## What existing Blender can actually do

The official [Displace documentation](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/modeling/modifiers/deform/displace.html) describes vertex movement by `(texture−midlevel)×strength`, with UV coordinates and a vertex-group influence. [Simple subdivision](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/modeling/modifiers/generate/subdivision_surface.html) adds geometry suitable for displacement without the intended smoothing of Catmull–Clark. This is different from [bump-only material displacement](https://docs.blender.org/manual/id/4.5/render/materials/components/displacement.html), which changes shading rather than the actual surface.

Use an evaluated **real mesh**, not only Cycles render-time adaptive displacement: retained outlines and collision must later exist in Godot. Ordinary Blender modifiers/topology and Cycles are sufficient; no new application, add-on or installation is justified. Actual local5.2 modifier behavior will still need validation in the authorized small run; this static task did not execute its API.

[Selected-to-Active baking](https://docs.blender.org/UATEST/manual/en/4.5/render/cycles/baking.html) transfers high-source shading to an active low mesh with an image target and UV. A cage must enclose the relevant source, and a custom cage retains the target topology. Baking normals does not create silhouette or collision; those shapes remain in the low mesh. Keep concrete/soil bake groups separate to prevent rays hitting the vertical slab instead of the adjacent ground. Albedo excludes light/shadow; AO stays in its separate channel.
